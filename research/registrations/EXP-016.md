# EXP-016 — Polymarket crypto windows: (a) resolution check, (b) fair-value engine calibration

Registered 2026-10-29 before any statistic below was computed. Plan: `docs/POLYMARKET_CRYPTO_PLAN.md`
(owner approved steps 1–3 on 2026-10-29). This experiment involves **no Polymarket price and no
trading rule**: it checks how markets resolve and builds the reference probability that later
experiments compare market prices against. The sealed holdout (2025-01-01 .. 2026-09-30) is not
loaded.

## EXP-016a — what the Chainlink "TWAP" rule means (5m / 15m / 4h)

Census 2026-10-29 (`research/results/PM-CENSUS/`): current 5m/15m/4h descriptions say Up if "the TWAP
of the time range is >= the price at the beginning of that range", source the Chainlink BTC/USD
TWAP-60s stream (`cryptoMarketConfig.twapLookbackSeconds = 60`); older 5m markets used the plain
BTC/USD stream, end price vs start price. Two readings of the current text:

- **I1 end-point**: trailing-60 s TWAP at window end >= trailing-60 s TWAP at window start.
- **I2 window-average**: mean of the stream over the whole window >= stream value at window start.
- I0 (control): spot at end >= spot at start.

Data: resolved BTC 15m and 4h markets ending 2026-10-20 00:00Z .. 2026-10-28 00:00Z (Gamma, by slug;
outcome = settled `outcomePrices`), every third 5m market in the same span; Binance spot BTCUSDT 1 s
klines as the proxy for the Chainlink stream (Chainlink is an aggregate, so near-zero moves are
noisy). Statistic: on markets where I1 and I2 disagree and both margins exceed 2 bps, the share of
outcomes each reading matches. Decision: the reading matching >= 80 % of those markets (with >= 20 of
them) is adopted for EXP-016b and the collector; otherwise UNRESOLVED and TWAP families are left out
of 016b. Also reported: overall match rate of I0/I1/I2, and the date the TWAP wording first appears.

## EXP-016b — fair-value engine calibration on TRAIN / VALIDATION

Data: Binance spot BTCUSDT 1 m klines (public archive), TRAIN 2020-01-01 .. 2022-12-31 for fitting,
VALIDATION 2023-01-01 .. 2024-12-31 for the one evaluation. (5m/15m/4h use 1 m spot as the stream
proxy; 1h and 1d are the exact resolution series.)

Windows (all aligned as Polymarket aligns them): 15m, 1h, 4h, 1d noon-to-noon ET (I1 or I2 per
016a for 15m/4h). Decision points: elapsed fraction f in {0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8, 0.9}, rounded to the
minute. State at decision time t: x = ln(S_t / S_0) (and, for I2, the running average).

Models (3, fixed; no others will be run):
- **M0**: P(Up) = Φ(m / (σ̂ √τ)), σ̂ per minute = EWMA of 1 m log returns, half-life 60 min; τ minutes
  left; m = x (I1, 1h, 1d) or the I2 distance to the barrier with the averaging variance factor.
- **M1**: M0 with one vol multiplier k per window type, fitted on TRAIN by log loss.
- **M2**: M1 with Student-t innovations, df per window type fitted on TRAIN.

Selection: per window type, the model with the lowest TRAIN log loss. Only the selected model is
evaluated on VALIDATION.

Statistics on VALIDATION: Brier and log loss; reliability in probability bins (taking the favoured
side, so p in [0.5, 1]): [0.5,0.6) [0.6,0.7) [0.7,0.8) [0.8,0.9) [0.9,0.95) [0.95,0.98) [0.98,0.995);
per bin, error = hit rate − mean p with a 90 % day-block bootstrap CI (1,000 resamples).

Gate per window type, `CALIBRATED`: in every bin with n >= 500, the 90 % CI of the error intersects
[−1.0 pp, +1.0 pp]. Why 1 pp: at p = 0.95 the taker fee is 0.35 % of stake and the spread is about
0.5–1 pp; a reference price that is off by more than 1 pp cannot separate a market error from its
own. A window type that fails is not used as a reference in later experiments (no re-fitting on
VALIDATION).

## What this cannot show
Whether any Polymarket price is wrong (needs the forward book collector, `pmc.collector`, and a
separate registration once about 3 weeks of books exist); Chainlink-vs-Binance basis on TWAP
windows; behaviour in 2025–26 (sealed).

## Prediction
016a: I1 matches (the 60 s TWAP is a manipulation guard at the two endpoints). Prior 70 %.
016b: 1h and 1d CALIBRATED with M1 or M2; 15m most likely to fail in the top bins (1 m proxy too
coarse). Prior that all four pass: 35 %.
