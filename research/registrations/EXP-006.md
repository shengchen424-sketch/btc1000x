# EXP-006 — Tail-shock reversal with open-interest flush (ShockFade)

Registered 2026-10-14 before any outcome was read. Source: LIT-001 item 2 (De Nicola 2021;
Miralles-Quirós 2022; arXiv 2608.21888 as the counter-evidence that only the tail is open).

## Hypothesis
A 1h bar whose absolute log return is >= 3 x the trailing 30-day hourly sigma, accompanied by a fall in
open interest over the same hour (forced liquidations), overshoots; fading it at the next 1m open earns
positive net R over 2–4 hours. Without the OI condition the effect is expected to be weaker (control).

## Rules (`src/b1k/strategies/event_fade.py::ShockFade`)
sigma = std of hourly log returns over the prior 720 bars (shifted, causal). Shock: |r_1h| >= 3 sigma.
OI condition: OI(last 5m snapshot closed at or before the bar close) / OI(last snapshot closed at or
before the bar open) - 1 <= -2 %. Binance metrics archive, 5m, from 2021-06-01 (so the usable sample
starts 2021-07 after the sigma warm-up); trials without the OI condition use the same start for
comparability. SHORT after an up-shock, LONG after a down-shock. Entry next 1m open; stop 1.5 %; time
exit after hold_bars x 1h. One position at a time.

## Trials: 4, fixed
oi_drop {0 (control), 0.02} x hold_bars {2, 4}. k_sigma 3, stop 1.5 %, window 720 fixed.

## Evaluation
TRAIN 2021-07 .. 2022-12 reported for context; gates on VALIDATION 2023–24.
CANDIDATE: n >= 30, mean net R > 0, bootstrap CI95 lower > 0, p < 0.05/4, both years positive, max
trade <= 20 % of gross profit. LEAD: n >= 30, mean > 0, p < 0.05, both years positive. A 1.5 % stop is
outside 10 USDT feasibility at 1 % risk (noted; a LEAD is observation-only).

## Failure criterion
No CANDIDATE or LEAD -> closed; no k / OI / hold / stop variants.
