# EXP-004 — Strategy D: daily-close trend rules on the perpetual with funding (position-based)

Registered 2026-10-14 before any VALIDATION outcome was read. Prior exposure: the owner's earlier
project found T7 (SMA200 filter + 55/20 breakout) passes on SPOT 2018–2025 and fails on the PERP in
DEV because of funding paid while long. That is a known result on overlapping history; this
registration re-tests it with this project's cost model, on both sides, and adds the mirrors. Any pass
here is discovery-level evidence at best.

## Hypothesis

Multi-week BTC trends persist long enough that a daily-close breakout with a trend filter earns more
than fees plus the funding paid while holding; on the short side funding is usually received.

## Rules (`src/b1k/strategies/daily_trend.py`, simulator `backtest/position_sim.py`)

Decided on the 00:00 UTC daily close, executed at the next open, 1.0x notional, no stop other than the
rule's exit. Fees 6 bps + 3 bps slippage per position change; realised funding on the held notional.

## Trials: 6, fixed

T7_L, T7_S (N 200, entry 55, exit 20), DON_L, DON_S (55/20), SMA200_L, SMA200_S. No neighbours.

## Evaluation (daily returns, VALIDATION 2023-01-01 .. 2024-12-31, 731 days)

CANDIDATE: CAGR > 0 with Sharpe >= 1.0, one-sided t-test on daily net returns p < 0.05/6, both years
positive, MAR >= 0.5. LEAD: Sharpe >= 0.7, p < 0.05, both years positive. Baselines reported: buy and
hold perp (funding paid) and flat. A long rule must also beat buy-and-hold on MAR to be CANDIDATE.
Feasibility: a position-based rule has no fixed stop, so the 1 % risk rule cannot size it; at 10 USDT
one lot (8.4 USDT) is a 0.84x notional position with the rule's typical adverse excursion of 10–20 %
as the risk: outside RISK defaults. Reported for information; live use would need an explicit owner
decision on risk per trade.

## Failure criterion

No CANDIDATE or LEAD -> the family is closed on this history.
