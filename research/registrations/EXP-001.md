# EXP-001 — Baseline: Strategy A (failed break) and Strategy B (trend pullback), BTCUSDT perpetual

Registered 2026-10-14 before any VALIDATION outcome was read. Git commit of this registration is the
one that adds this file; the run records `git rev-parse HEAD` in `research/results/EXP-001/verdict.json`.

## Disclosure of prior exposure

- During engine debugging, the four DEFAULT parameter sets (A long/short, B long/short) were run once on
  calendar year 2022 (inside TRAIN). Observed: all four negative net R; costs 0.15–0.45 R per trade.
  The grid below was fixed after that without reference to those numbers other than the decision to
  report cost share prominently. VALIDATION (2023–2024) and HOLDOUT (2025–2026-09) have not been read.
- Smart Trader (owner's earlier project, separate repository) tested liquidity-sweep/reclaim, range
  reclaim, OTE pullback and BTC 1h–12h trend systems on Binance-proxied 2020–2026 data and found no
  edge after costs. This project's rules differ in definition, but the hypothesis families are not new
  to this history. Any positive result here is therefore DISCOVERY evidence only; only the forward shadow
  record can confirm.

## Hypotheses

- H_A: a break of a prior 15m swing level that is reclaimed within 2 bars is followed, more often than
  costs require, by a move of >= 1.5–3 R back into the range within 6 hours (long on failed breakdown,
  short on failed breakout).
- H_B: in a 4h trend (EMA50 slope and side), a 1h pullback to within 0.5–1.0 ATR of EMA20 that resumes
  (close above the prior bar high) continues far enough that a 2–3 ATR chandelier trail captures > 0 R
  net after costs (long and short mirrored).
- H_R: a transparent 4h regime label improves the pooled expected utility of A and B on VALIDATION
  relative to taking every trade (router value).

## Data

- Binance USDT-M BTCUSDT 1m klines, 2020-01-01 .. 2026-10-13 (data.binance.vision; manifests with
  sha256 in `data/manifests/`); funding rates from the same archive. Bitget parity measured on
  2026-08..10: close p99 |diff| 6.6 bps, return correlation 0.999 (`evidence/exchange/bitget_vs_binance_parity.json`).
- Availability: a bar is usable only after its close_time; HTF bars attach by close_time <= signal
  close_time (`strategies.base.align_htf`); regime label = last closed 4h bar before the signal.
- Splits (`config.Splits`): TRAIN 2020-01-01..2022-12-31; VALIDATION 2023-01-01..2024-12-31;
  HOLDOUT 2025-01-01..2026-09-30 sealed (not loaded by this experiment); FORWARD from 2026-10-14.
- Order-book, liquidation and open-interest history are NOT used (not available at the needed resolution).

## Rules (exact code: `src/b1k/strategies/failed_break.py`, `trend_pullback.py`, engine `backtest/engine.py`)

Entry: market at the open of the first 1m bar after the signal bar closes. Exits checked on 1m bars.
Stop and take-profit in the same 1m bar -> stop. Gap through a stop -> fill at the open.

## Trials: 24, fixed

| Strategy | Fixed | Grid |
|---|---|---|
| A FAILED_BREAK, 15m | reclaim_bars 2, stop_atr 0.25, max_hold 24 bars (6 h) | side {LONG, SHORT} x lookback {48, 96} x rr {1.5, 3.0} x vol_mult {0, 1.5} = 16 |
| B TREND_PULLBACK, 1h/4h | pb_window 6, stop_atr 0.5, max_hold 48 bars | side {LONG, SHORT} x pb_depth {0.5, 1.0} x trail_atr {2.0, 3.0} = 8 |

No other parameter will be run under this experiment id.

## Costs (`config.CostModel`, verified Bitget rules 2026-10-14)

Taker 6 bps per side (all fills are market/trigger); slippage 3 bps entry, 10 bps on stop/TP triggers,
3 bps on time exits; realised funding on the entry notional at each 8h settlement during the hold.
Results are in R (planned loss). Cost in R rises as the stop tightens: at a 0.5 % stop the round trip
is ~0.5 R. This is reported per trial as `mean_cost_r`.

## Risk model

One position at a time. Sizing is a separate layer: `metrics.account_sim_one_lot` replays each trial
on a 10 USDT account where every trade is exactly one lot (0.0001 BTC) if one lot's planned loss fits
1 % of equity, else skipped.

## Evaluation and gates (VALIDATION only; TRAIN is used for regime eligibility and diagnostics)

Per trial on VALIDATION:
- CANDIDATE (may go to forward shadow as a champion): n >= 60; mean net R > 0; bootstrap 95 % CI lower
  bound > 0; one-sided p < 0.05 / 24 = 0.00208; both calendar years positive; largest trade <= 20 % of
  gross profits; mean stop_frac <= 0.91 % (10 USDT feasibility).
- LEAD (forward shadow observation only, no champion status): n >= 60; mean net R > 0; p < 0.05;
  both years positive.
- Otherwise FAIL.

Router (H_R): eligibility learned on TRAIN (`portfolio.router.learn_eligibility`, min_n 30, p < 0.10).
Routed VALIDATION trades must have n >= 60 and mean net R above BOTH baselines (all 24 trials pooled
unfiltered; best single trial by TRAIN mean) for the router to count as adding value. Otherwise the
router is reported as NOT adding value and the Phase 2 default is NO_TRADE outside directly validated
trials.

## Failure criteria for the experiment as a whole

If no trial is CANDIDATE or LEAD: Phase 1 concludes "no executable edge found in these two families
under these costs"; the recommendation must not be a wider grid on the same data.
