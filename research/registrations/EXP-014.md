# EXP-014 — Cross-sectional relative value, hours-to-days horizon: residual momentum and carry-adjusted momentum

Registered 2026-10-14 before any outcome was read. Owner framework week 3: test mechanisms that are
different from the taker-flow factor, and ask whether they are independent of it (combination value),
not whether they beat it. Prior art (failure index): plain 7-day cross-sectional momentum on this
universe FAILED (Smart Trader batch 7 D1: OOS Sharpe 0.4, top-3 days carry it). Residual momentum and
carry-adjusted momentum are distinct mechanisms (market-hedged idiosyncratic continuation; expected
return net of the funding paid to hold the position).

## Common engine (identical to the reproduced flow book, only the ranking score changes)
Universe top 30 by 30-day median quote volume, listed >= 180 days; weekly rebalance, 5 longs = first 5
of the top-10 scores, 5 shorts = first 5 of the bottom-10; equal weight, gross 1.0; 10 bps turnover;
realised funding; 2x trim; 7 offsets pooled. `research/experiments/exp014_relative_value.py`.

## Scores (fixed; no other lookbacks will be run)
- **RM7 / RM14 residual momentum**: daily log return minus beta x BTC daily log return, beta from a
  trailing 90-day regression (min 60 days); score = sum of residuals over the last 7 (resp. 14) days.
- **CAM14 carry-adjusted momentum**: RM14 minus the expected funding cost of holding the position for
  the coming week, 21 x trailing 7-day mean per-settlement funding rate (a long pays positive
  funding; the score for a short candidate is symmetric by construction of the ranking).
- **CARRY (control, not a candidate)**: score = minus the trailing 7-day mean funding rate (long
  coins where shorts pay, short coins where longs pay). Smart Trader batch 7 D3 tested the same idea;
  it is run here only to measure its correlation with the flow book under identical execution.
Funding is available for the 180 symbols the flow book traded plus whatever the new books trade;
missing funding is treated as zero (disclosed).

## Evaluation
Independent window 2025-01-01 .. 2026-09-30; 2023–24 reported. For each candidate:
(1) standalone: annual return, Sharpe, max DD, one-sided p on daily returns, 7-day block-bootstrap CI;
(2) daily-return correlation with the flow book A (ST-AUDIT-001 reproduction);
(3) portfolio value: 50/50 mix with A at the same gross, paired against A (Sharpe and return
difference with CI).

## Gates (3 candidates, Bonferroni p < 0.0167)
INDEPENDENT-ALPHA: 2025–26 Sharpe >= 0.7 with p < 0.0167, 2023–24 Sharpe > 0, and corr(A) < 0.3.
LEAD: Sharpe >= 0.7, p < 0.05, corr < 0.3. Otherwise DISCARD. The 50/50 result decides nothing here;
it is input to the capital-allocation layer.

## Prediction
RM7 15 %, RM14 25 %, CAM14 25 % of reaching LEAD or better. CARRY expected to correlate > 0.3 with A
(both short crowded longs).
