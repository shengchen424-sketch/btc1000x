# EXP-009 — Sentiment and on-chain daily rules: Fear & Greed contrarian, exchange-flow z-score

Registered 2026-10-14 before any outcome was read. Owner asked for social-media information to be
used. What is obtainable with history and without bypassing access controls: the alternative.me
Crypto Fear & Greed index (daily since 2018-02; a composite of volatility, volume, social-media
activity, surveys, dominance and search trends) and CoinMetrics community on-chain data (daily,
including exchange inflow/outflow in USD). X/Twitter history is not freely available; Reddit, Google
Trends, GDELT and Wikipedia pageviews refused this session's shared egress address; Telegram public
channel previews are scrapeable but only for the latest ~20 messages (no history). Snapshots used:
`evidence/alt/fng_2018-02_2026-10-14.parquet`, `evidence/alt/coinmetrics_btc_2019-06_2026-05-24.parquet`.

## Point-in-time
F&G value stamped at 00:00 UTC of day D is used for the decision at D's close. CoinMetrics row for
day D is used only from the close of D+1 (one-day lag). `src/b1k/data/alt.py`.

## Rules (`src/b1k/strategies/daily_alt.py`)
fng_contrarian: F&G <= lo -> long until F&G >= lo_exit or 30 days; F&G >= hi -> short until F&G <= hi_exit or 30 days.
exflow_zscore: z of net exchange inflow (USD) over 90 days; z >= 2 -> short 5 days; z <= -2 -> long 5 days.

## Trials: 6
FNG_L (lo 20 / exit 40), FNG_L25 (lo 25 / exit 50), FNG_S (hi 80 / exit 60), FNG_S75 (hi 75 / exit 50),
EXFLOW_S (z >= 2 short 5d), EXFLOW_L (z <= -2 long 5d).

No CANDIDATE or LEAD -> closed. A LEAD goes to the shadow registry for forward observation (owner's
