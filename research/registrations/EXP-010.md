# EXP-010 — Maker-entry execution study, applied to Strategy B long (registered rescue attempt)

Registered 2026-10-14 before any outcome was read. Disclosure: Strategy B (trend pullback) FAILED
EXP-001 on VALIDATION and its family was closed for parameter, timeframe and filter variants. This
experiment changes nothing in the signal; it changes the execution assumption, which the owner has
authorised as a path ("tune, then forward OOS"). It is a rescue attempt and is labelled as such.
A positive result cannot be promoted on in-sample statistics; it can only enter forward shadow,
where the fill assumption itself is what gets tested.

## Execution model under test
Entry: a passive limit order at the signal bar's close price, placed at the bar close, cancelled if
not filled within the next 60 minutes (the next 1h bar). Fill rule on 1m bars (conservative): filled
at the limit if some 1m bar's LOW is strictly below the limit (a touch is not a fill); the first such
minute is the entry time. Maker fee 2 bps, no entry slippage. Exits unchanged (taker, trigger
slippage). Everything else as EXP-001.

## Trials: 4 (the four B long configurations of EXP-001)
pb_depth {0.5, 1.0} x trail_atr {2.0, 3.0}; side LONG only (B short lost before costs).

## Statistics reported
Fill rate; net R on filled trades under maker costs; the same trades' net R under EXP-001's taker
model (to isolate the execution effect); selection check: mean net R of UNFILLED signals under the
taker model (if unfilled signals were the winners, the limit order selects against itself).

## Gates (VALIDATION, filled trades)
LEAD: n >= 60, mean net R > 0, p < 0.05, both years positive, and the unfilled-signal taker mean
is not higher than the filled-signal maker mean by more than 0.1 R (no adverse selection).
CANDIDATE: additionally CI95 lower > 0 and p < 0.0125. Feasibility note: B's stops are 1.3–1.6 %,
beyond 10 USDT at 1 % risk; a LEAD is observation-only.

## Failure criterion
No LEAD -> the execution rescue is closed too; Strategy B stays closed.
