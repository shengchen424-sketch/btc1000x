# EXP-013 — Incremental alpha on the Smart Trader flow book: three features, paired B − A

Registered 2026-10-14 after ST-AUDIT-001 and before any B − A statistic was computed. Framework
(owner, 2026-10-14): a feature is adopted only if the strategy WITH it beats the same strategy WITHOUT
it on the same days, with a confidence interval, on an independent sample.

## Baseline A
The reproduced research-form book (`research/experiments/st_audit_book.py`, 7 offsets pooled, 10 bps
turnover cost, realised funding, 2x trim). Daily net series `daily.parquet`.

## Disclosure
ST-AUDIT-001 looked at 2025–26 in aggregate (leg sums by year, BTC-drawdown regime split, decay). The
three features below were chosen from those aggregate observations; their exact definitions were not
fitted to any data. The 2025-01 .. 2026-09 window is therefore "independent of the book's discovery
(2020–24)" but not "unseen by the auditor": the registered Bonferroni and the requirement of the same
sign in 2023–24 are the controls for that.

## Features (fixed definitions, no tuning)
- **F1a BTC near-high gate, long leg**: on rebalance days when BTC close >= 0.95 x its trailing
  90-day high, long weights are 0.05 instead of 0.10 (short leg unchanged; book net short 0.25).
- **F1b BTC near-high gate, both legs**: same condition, both legs 0.05 (neutral, half gross).
- **F2a Short-crowding filter**: a short candidate whose trailing 7-day mean funding rate is below
  -0.05 % per 8h (shorts paying heavily) is skipped and the next bottom-ranked coin is taken.
- **F2b Long-crowding filter**: a long candidate whose trailing 7-day mean funding is above +0.05 %
  per 8h is skipped and the next top-ranked coin is taken.
- **F3 ETH/BTC alt-regime gate, long leg**: when ETH/BTC's 30-day return is negative at the rebalance,
  long weights 0.05 (short unchanged).
Funding is known only for the 180 symbols the baseline traded; a candidate without funding data is
treated as not crowded (disclosed limitation).

## Statistic
d_t = net_B,t − net_A,t, pooled over the 7 offsets, on 2025-01-01 .. 2026-09-30 (638 days). Report:
mean annualised difference, 7-day block-bootstrap CI95 (5,000 draws), one-sided p; the same on
2023-01 .. 2024-12 for sign consistency; and the drawdown and Sharpe of B.

## Gates (5 trials, Bonferroni p < 0.01)
ADOPT-CANDIDATE: CI95 of the 2025–26 annualised difference excludes 0 on the positive side, p < 0.01,
and the 2023–24 difference has the same sign. LEAD: p < 0.05 and same sign. Otherwise DISCARD.
A feature that improves Sharpe/drawdown but lowers return is reported, not adopted (the owner's
criterion is net return; drawdown control is a separate allocation decision).

## Prediction
F1a/F1b: likely to lower return (near-high periods still earn +24 %/yr) while cutting drawdown ->
DISCARD by the return criterion. F2a: 30 %. F2b: 25 %. F3: 20 %.
