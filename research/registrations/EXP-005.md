# EXP-005 — Options-expiry 08:00 UTC reversal (ExpiryFade)

Registered 2026-10-14 before any outcome was read (TRAIN or VALIDATION). Source: LIT-001 item 1
(Weiss et al., FRL 2026, 2021–2023 sample).

## Hypothesis
On Deribit expiry days the 07:00–08:00 UTC move is partly dealer hedging and reverts after 08:00.
Fading the 07:00->08:00 move at the 08:00 close earns a positive net R over the next 90–240 minutes.

## Rules (`src/b1k/strategies/event_fade.py::ExpiryFade`)
Signal: 15m bar closing 08:00 UTC on an event day; move = close(08:00)/close(07:00) - 1; trade only if
|move| >= 0.10 %; SHORT after a rise, LONG after a fall. Entry next 1m open; stop 1.0 %; no target;
time exit after hold_bars x 15m. Costs as EXP-001 (a 1 % stop makes the round trip ~0.25 R).

## Trials: 4, fixed
events {monthly (last Friday), weekly (every Friday)} x hold_bars {6 (90 min), 16 (240 min)}.

## Data and evaluation
Binance UM 1m 2020-01 .. 2024-12. TRAIN 2020–22 is reported for context (the paper's sample overlaps it);
gates on VALIDATION 2023–24. Monthly events give ~24 VALIDATION signals at most, weekly ~100.
CANDIDATE: n >= 20 (monthly) / 40 (weekly), mean net R > 0, bootstrap CI95 lower > 0, p < 0.05/4, both
years positive, max trade <= 20 % of gross profit, mean stop_frac <= 0.91 %. LEAD: n as above, mean > 0,
p < 0.05, both years positive. The 1 % stop is just above the 0.91 % feasibility line; a LEAD would
need a 0.9 % stop variant registered separately before any shadow use.

## Failure criterion
No CANDIDATE or LEAD -> closed; no threshold / hold / stop variants.
