# EXP-002 — Strategy C: funding-rate extreme reversal with carry (BTCUSDT perpetual)

Registered 2026-10-14 before any VALIDATION outcome was read. Hypothesis family not previously run in
this repository; the owner's earlier project tested funding as a cross-sectional carry book and as a
single-asset "level predicts nothing" check on 2020–2026, not as a settlement-time extreme rule.

## Hypothesis

After a funding settlement whose rate is in the top tail of its trailing 90-day distribution (270
settlements), the next 24–72 h return is negative on average and the short also collects funding;
mirrored for the bottom tail. Mechanism: crowded leveraged positioning paying an extreme carry is
fragile (forced de-leveraging) and the carry itself is income for the other side.

## Rules (`src/b1k/strategies/funding_extreme.py`, engine `backtest/engine.py`)

Signal at the 1h bar closing on a settlement time; rate = the one settled at that time (Binance
archive, funding_time == close_time); percentile over the trailing 270 settlements, causal (rolling
rank). SHORT if percentile >= P and rate > 0; LONG if percentile <= 1-P and rate < 0. Entry next 1m
open; fixed stop 3 % of entry; no target; time exit after hold_h hours; one position at a time.

## Trials: 8, fixed

side {LONG, SHORT} x P {0.90, 0.97} x hold_h {24, 72}. stop 3 %, window 270 fixed.

## Costs, data, splits

As EXP-001 (taker both sides, 3/10/3 bps slippage, realised funding on entry notional). Binance UM 1m
and funding 2020-01 .. 2024-12; TRAIN 2020–22 (diagnostics only), VALIDATION 2023–24 (gates), HOLDOUT
sealed. Cost per trade at a 3 % stop is about 0.08 R plus funding (which is the bet, not a cost).

## Gates (VALIDATION), as EXP-001 with Bonferroni over 8 trials (p < 0.00625)

CANDIDATE: n >= 40 (fewer settlements qualify than 15m bars; 40 is the minimum for a CI to mean
anything here), mean net R > 0, bootstrap CI95 lower > 0, p < 0.00625, both years positive, max trade
<= 20 % of gross profits. LEAD: n >= 40, mean > 0, p < 0.05, both years positive. Feasibility note:
a 3 % stop on one lot is 0.25 USDT = 2.5 % of a 10 USDT account; the 10 USDT replay will therefore
skip every trade at 1 % risk. A 300 USDT replay is reported alongside for information only.

## Failure criterion

No CANDIDATE or LEAD -> the family is closed; no further percentile / hold / stop variants.
