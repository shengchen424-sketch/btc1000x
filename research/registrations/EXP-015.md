# EXP-015 — Incremental test: flow ranking blended with 14-day residual momentum (B − A)

Registered 2026-10-14 after EXP-014 was read and before any blended book was run. Mechanism: the
flow factor selects coins with one-sided taker demand over 7 days; residual momentum over 14 days
measures whether that demand has already moved the price idiosyncratically. Blending the two
rankings may (a) reinforce (pick coins where demand and price agree) or (b) double-count. EXP-014
showed RM14 is significant standalone with corr 0.47 to the flow book; whether it ADDS to the flow
book is the question here.

## Trials: 2, fixed
- **BLEND50**: score = z(flow) + z(RM14), both z-scored cross-sectionally within the day's universe.
- **BLEND25**: score = z(flow) + 0.5 z(RM14) (flow-dominant).
No other weights will be run. Everything else identical to baseline A.

## Statistic and gates (as EXP-013)
Paired d_t = net_B − net_A, 7 offsets pooled, independent window 2025-01-01 .. 2026-09-30, block
bootstrap CI95, one-sided p; sign check on 2023–24. ADOPT-CANDIDATE: CI95 lower > 0, p < 0.025
(Bonferroni 2), same sign in 2023–24. LEAD: p < 0.05 and same sign. Otherwise DISCARD.

## Prediction
EXP-013 showed the book's return cannot be resolved to ±10 %/yr in 638 days; a blend has a real chance
only if it changes the tail positions. Prior for ADOPT-CANDIDATE: 20 %; for LEAD: 35 %.
