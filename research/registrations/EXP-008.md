# EXP-008 — Daily volatility-compression breakout (position-based)

Registered 2026-10-14 before any outcome was read. LIT-001: no rigorous published backtest exists;
this is the last untested daily family with an economic story (range contraction precedes expansion;
a breakout after contraction has a better stop location than a breakout from a wide range).

## Rule (`src/b1k/strategies/daily_alt.py::compression_breakout`)
Bollinger width = 20-day close std / 20-day mean. Compression = width at or below its q-quantile over
the trailing 100 days, on any of the last 5 closed days. Long on a close above the prior 20-day high
while compressed-recently (mirror: short on a close below the prior 20-day low). Exit on a close below
the prior 10-day low (mirror: above the 10-day high) or after 20 days. 1.0x notional; fees 9 bps per
change; realised funding.

## Trials: 4
q {0.10, 0.20} x side {LONG, SHORT}.

## Evaluation
TRAIN 2020–22 for context; gates on VALIDATION 2023–24 as EXP-004: CANDIDATE CAGR > 0, Sharpe >= 1.0,
p < 0.05/4, both years positive, MAR >= 0.5 (and for longs MAR > buy-and-hold); LEAD Sharpe >= 0.7,
p < 0.05, both years positive.
