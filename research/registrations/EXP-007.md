# EXP-007 — FOMC pre-announcement drift and announcement-day reversal (position-based)

Registered 2026-10-14 before any outcome was read. Source: LIT-001 item 6 (Pyo & Lee 2020; pre-2020
sample). Dates: `evidence/alt/fomc_statement_dates.json`, parsed from federalreserve.gov on
2026-10-14 (statement links; 8 scheduled statements a year, plus listed unscheduled ones).

## Trials: 3
PRE_LONG (long during D-1), DAY_SHORT (short during D), BOTH. 1.0x notional, fees 9 bps per change,
realised funding. `src/b1k/strategies/fomc.py`.

## Evaluation
The Fed page lists 2021 onward, so TRAIN covers 2021–22 (16 statements), reported for context; gates
on VALIDATION 2023–24 (16 statements).
Active-day returns are few, so the daily t-test over all days is the registered statistic.
CANDIDATE: CAGR > 0, Sharpe >= 1.0, p < 0.05/3, both years positive. LEAD: Sharpe >= 0.7, p < 0.05,
both years positive. Prediction: with 16 events, even a true +0.5 % per event (Sharpe ~1 on active
days) gives p ~ 0.1; the likely outcome is "underpowered", which will be reported as such, not as a
lead.

## Failure criterion
No CANDIDATE or LEAD -> closed. No window / horizon variants.
