# EXP-012 — BTC order-flow shocks at 1-minute resolution (taker imbalance, trade intensity, OI)

Registered 2026-10-14 before any outcome was read. Owner priority 1: "liquidation and order-flow
anomalies: short-horizon, repeatable price dislocations". Constraints from the owner's upgraded rules:
PnL from prices AFTER the signal only; fees, slippage, funding and liquidation distance included;
no repackaging of falsified families (EXP-001 A, EXP-006 shock fade are closed and are not re-run).

## Data (point-in-time, public)
- Binance UM BTCUSDT 1m klines (already held): per-bar `taker_buy_volume`, `volume`, `count` (trades).
  Order-flow imbalance OFI = (2 x taker_buy - volume) / volume per bar; intensity = count.
- Binance 5m metrics (held, 2021-06 ..): open interest; the 5m snapshot closed at or before the
  signal minute.
- Liquidation prints are NOT public historically (archive removed); the OI drop is the proxy, as in
  EXP-006. A forward `forceOrder` websocket collector is a separate deliverable, not part of this test.

## Mechanism tested
A one-minute burst of one-sided taker flow with abnormal trade count is forced flow (stop cascades,
liquidation engines, large market orders). Two competing hypotheses are registered as separate
trial groups, so that neither is a parameter variant of the other:
- H_cont (continuation): forced flow precedes further movement over the next 15 minutes
  (information or cascade continuation). Trade WITH the flow.
- H_rev (reversal): forced flow overshoots and reverts within 60 minutes. Trade AGAINST the flow.

## Signal definition (5m clock, 1m exits)
On each closed 5m bar: OFI5 = sum of signed taker flow over the 5 bars / total volume; INT5 = trade
count over the 5 bars / trailing 7-day median of 5-bar counts. Signal when |OFI5| >= q_ofi (trailing
30-day 99th percentile of |OFI5|) AND INT5 >= 3. Direction = sign(OFI5). Optional OI condition:
OI change over the same 5 minutes <= -0.5 % (forced closing).

## Trials: 8, fixed
H_cont x hold {15 min, 60 min} x OI {none, drop}; H_rev x hold {15 min, 60 min} x OI {none, drop}.
Stop 0.6 % (feasible at 10 USDT at 1 % risk; round-trip cost ~0.4 R), no take-profit, time exit.
Entry at the next 1m open (taker), engine as EXP-001.

## Splits and gates
TRAIN 2021-07 .. 2022-12 reported; gates on VALIDATION 2023–24; HOLDOUT sealed (unopened for this
family). CANDIDATE: n >= 60, mean net R > 0, bootstrap CI95 lower > 0, p < 0.05/8, both years
positive, max trade <= 20 % of gross profits. LEAD: n >= 60, mean > 0, p < 0.05, both years positive.

## Prediction, written before the run
Published microstructure evidence (LIT-001 item 5; arXiv 2608.21888) puts unconditional
sub-hour effects at < 2 bps; a 0.6 % stop makes the round trip ~0.4 R, so a trial needs a gross edge
of roughly 0.25 % of price per event to pass. Prior that any trial reaches LEAD: 15 %.

## Failure criterion
No CANDIDATE or LEAD -> the order-flow family is closed on public data; the only remaining version
of this line is forward collection of the liquidation stream and the order book on the VPS.
