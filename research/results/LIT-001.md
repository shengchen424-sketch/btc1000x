# LIT-001 — Literature scan: documented BTC perpetual return anomalies 2022–2026 (2026-10-14)

Produced by a web-research pass; sources are listed inline. Used to choose Phase 2 round-2 mechanisms.
Cost frame: round trip ~18–32 bps taker or ~10–15 bps maker; every sub-hour microstructure anomaly
found is < 2 bps gross, so only event-conditional, multi-hour trades can clear the bar.

Data facts checked: Binance `futures/um/daily/metrics` (5-min OI, top-trader L/S, taker ratio) is free
(this repository holds it from 2021-06); `liquidationSnapshot` was removed from data.binance.vision
(github.com/binance/binance-public-data/issues/337).

## Ranked candidates

1. **Deribit options-expiry 08:00 UTC reversal** — Weiss, Gaudiosi, Zhou & Webb, Finance Research
   Letters 107 (2026): dealer gamma hedging pushes price away from the settlement level into 08:00 and
   it snaps back within ~90 min, strongest on high ATM-OI days. Peer-reviewed; 2021–2023 sample; no
   2024–26 out-of-sample; no cost analysis. Free data: expiry calendar + 1m klines. -> **EXP-005**.
2. **Tail-shock + OI-flush reversal at 1–4h** — De Nicola, Ledger 6 (2021): 1h/2h/4h return
   autocorrelation -0.06 to -0.09, p < 1e-6, larger moves revert more; Miralles-Quirós (2022):
   overreaction after negative hourly shocks. Counter: arXiv 2608.21888 (2025–26, 183 pairs, frozen
   holdout): unconditional reversal 1.3 bp gross vs 5 bp cheapest round trip, dead; only the tail
   version is open. Free data: klines + metrics OI. -> **EXP-006**.
3. **Spot-ETF flow -> next-day return** — Lim, SSRN 6592830 (2026): flows predict next-day returns
   (t 3.1) then reverse; OKX finds next-day correlation only +0.08. Data only from 2024-01 (Farside).
   Preprint, 16-month in-sample. -> deferred (no TRAIN/VAL split possible yet).
4. **Funding/basis extremes as a crash filter, not an entry** — BIS WP 1087; arXiv 2510.14435 (carry
   Sharpe fell from 6.5 to negative in 2025); directional fades flat after fees (tradingstrategies.work
   backtest, 93 trades). Consistent with EXP-002's failure. Possible future filter for EXP-004 LEADs,
   only via a new registration.
5. **Quarter-hour turn-of-candle effect** — Shanaev et al., Heliyon 2023; Kim & Hansen arXiv 2607.09426:
   real, +0.58 bps/min at boundaries, zero as a strategy at 12+ bps costs. Use as execution hygiene:
   place maker entries before boundaries, avoid taker fills at them.
6. **FOMC pre-announcement drift** — Pyo & Lee 2020 (+0.96 % day before, -1 % on the day), 8 events a
   year, pre-2020 sample, untested since. Cheap to test later.

## Rejected
CME gap fills (blog-level, and CME went 24/7 on 2026-05-29); funding settlement-time effect
(undocumented; Kim & Hansen find nothing at settlement quarter-hours); 22:00–23:00 UTC seasonality
(cost drag 35–50 %/yr, no OOS); weekend/Monday (no gap 2014–2024); Coinbase/Kimchi premium (no
out-of-sample predictive study); stablecoin supply (reverse causality); OI-flush / L/S ratio / taker
imbalance alone (commentary only; folded into EXP-006 as a filter); daily volatility-compression
breakout (no rigorous backtest exists; cheap to test later).

Bottom line of the scan: nothing documented clears the cost hurdle unconditionally. Candidates 1 and
2 have a mechanism, peer-reviewed evidence and free data, and lack 2024–26 confirmation: exactly what
VALIDATION (2023–24) and the sealed holdout are for.
