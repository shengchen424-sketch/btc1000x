## Limits
- Same machine, same library versions: this does not show portability to another environment.
- The reproduction regenerates the committed CSVs; the original run's console log is in the session
  scratchpad only (not in git).
- A second independent run of `st_audit2_paths.py` (run-to-run determinism of the paths files) was not
  performed; the in-process equality assertion covers the equity path, not the parquet writing.
